## Workout Overview

Hey everyone! Today’s WOD is all about quality movement and building endurance. We’re going to tackle 6 rounds of rowing or skiing, followed by some box step-ups, kettlebell sumo deadlift high pulls, and ring rows. This workout is designed to improve your cardiovascular fitness, leg strength, and upper body pulling power. Remember, since we’re focusing on quality, take your time with each movement and ensure you’re maintaining good form throughout.

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Start by sitting on the rower or ski erg with your feet securely strapped in.
    - For rowing, grab the handle with both hands, keeping your back straight and core engaged.
    - Push through your legs, lean back slightly at the hips, and pull the handle towards your chest, finishing with your elbows down and back.
    - For the ski erg, hold the handles with both hands, engage your core, and use your legs to drive down while pulling the handles down towards your hips.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and avoid rounding your shoulders.
    - Use your legs to initiate the movement; your arms should follow.
    - Maintain a steady rhythm; don’t rush!

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or even 1:30 to focus on technique.
    - You can also substitute with a stationary bike or a brisk walk if needed.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with your feet shoulder-width apart.
    - Step up onto the box with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body up.
    - Bring your other foot up to meet the first on the box, then step back down one foot at a time.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core throughout the movement.
    - Ensure your entire foot is on the box to maintain balance.
    - Alternate legs for each rep to ensure even development.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights or use a dumbbell for added resistance.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out.
    - Place the kettlebell between your feet and hinge at the hips to grab the handle with both hands.
    - Drive through your heels, extending your hips and knees, and pull the kettlebell up towards your chin, keeping your elbows high.
    - Lower the kettlebell back down to the ground in a controlled manner.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and chest up during the lift.
    - Use your legs to drive the movement; your arms should be relaxed until the kettlebell reaches your chin.
    - Avoid rounding your back; focus on a strong hip extension.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell or perform the movement with a dumbbell.
    - If you’re new to this movement, practice the deadlift first without the high pull.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height where you can comfortably reach them while standing.
    - Grab the rings with an overhand grip, lean back, and keep your body straight.
    - Pull your chest towards the rings by bending your elbows and squeezing your shoulder blades together.
    - Lower yourself back down in a controlled manner.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
    - Avoid using momentum; focus on a controlled pull.
    - Engage your core to maintain stability.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows on a barbell or use a TRX.
    - Adjust the angle of your body to make it easier or harder.
