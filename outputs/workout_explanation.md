## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps**:
    - **Rowing**: Sit on the rower, strap your feet in, and grab the handle. Start with your knees bent and back straight. Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest, keeping your elbows close.
    - **Ski Erg**: Stand tall, grab the handles, and start with your arms extended. Use your legs to push off while pulling the handles down towards your hips, engaging your core throughout.
  
- **Form Tips**:
    - Keep your back straight and avoid rounding your shoulders.
    - For rowing, focus on driving with your legs first, then lean back and pull.
    - For Ski Erg, engage your core and use your legs to drive the movement, not just your arms.

- **Scaling/Modifications**:
    - If you're new to rowing, aim for a slower pace to focus on technique.
    - For Ski Erg, reduce the duration to 2:00 if needed.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps**:
    - Stand facing the box with feet hip-width apart. Step up onto the box with one foot, pressing through your heel to stand tall on the box. Step back down and repeat on the other leg.
  
- **Form Tips**:
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core.
    - Make sure your entire foot is on the box to avoid slipping.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications**:
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights to focus on balance and form.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps**:
    - Stand with feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out. Hold the kettlebell with both hands between your legs. Hinge at the hips, keeping your back straight, and grab the kettlebell. 
    - Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up to your chin, keeping your elbows high.
  
- **Form Tips**:
    - Keep your back flat and chest up during the hinge.
    - Use your legs to initiate the movement, not just your arms.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications**:
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you're new to this movement.
    - If kettlebells are unavailable, you can substitute with dumbbells.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps**:
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to lean back with your arms extended. Grab the rings, lean back, and pull your chest towards the rings while keeping your body straight.
  
- **Form Tips**:
    - Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
    - Engage your core and avoid sagging your hips.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications**:
    - If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows using a barbell or a low bar.
    - Adjust the height of the rings to make the movement easier or harder.
