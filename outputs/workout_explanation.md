## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - For the Row: Sit on the rower with your feet secured. Grab the handle with both hands, keeping your back straight and core engaged.
    - Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest, keeping your elbows close to your body.
    - Reverse the motion by extending your arms first, then leaning forward, and finally bending your knees to return to the starting position.
    - For the Ski Erg: Stand tall, grasp the handles with both hands. Engage your core and pull down with your arms while driving your knees slightly forward.
    - Use your legs to assist in the pull, keeping a steady rhythm.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and avoid rounding your shoulders.
    - Use your legs to generate power; your arms should follow.
    - Maintain a steady pace; don’t go all out at the start.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or 1:30.
    - Focus on technique rather than speed; it’s better to row slower with good form than fast with poor form.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand in front of the box with your feet hip-width apart.
    - Step up with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body onto the box.
    - Bring your other foot up to meet the first, then step back down one foot at a time.
    - Alternate legs with each rep.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and core engaged throughout the movement.
    - Ensure your entire foot is on the box before stepping down.
    - Use your arms for balance if needed.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re new to this movement or have mobility issues.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights to focus on form.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes pointed slightly out.
    - Place the kettlebell between your feet. Hinge at your hips and bend your knees to grab the kettlebell with both hands.
    - Drive through your heels, extending your hips and knees while pulling the kettlebell up to your chin, keeping your elbows high.
    - Lower the kettlebell back down to the ground in a controlled manner.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and chest up during the lift.
    - Use your legs to initiate the movement; your arms should follow.
    - Avoid rounding your back; hinge at the hips instead.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re new to this movement or focus on the deadlift portion without the high pull.
    - You can also perform the movement with a dumbbell if kettlebells are not available.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to pull yourself up while keeping your body straight.
    - Grab the rings with an overhand grip and lean back, keeping your body straight from head to heels.
    - Pull your chest towards the rings, squeezing your shoulder blades together at the top.
    - Lower yourself back down in a controlled manner.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your core tight and avoid sagging your hips.
    - Focus on pulling with your back, not just your arms.
    - Maintain a straight line from your head to your heels throughout the movement.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows on a barbell or use a TRX strap.
    - Adjust the height of the rings to make the movement easier or harder.
