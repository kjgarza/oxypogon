## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - **Rowing:** Sit on the rower with your feet secured. Start with your knees bent and arms extended. Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest, keeping your elbows close.
    - **Ski Erg:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding the handles. Engage your core, and pull the handles down towards your hips while simultaneously bending your knees slightly.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and core engaged throughout the movement.
    - For rowing, focus on driving with your legs first, then leaning back and pulling with your arms.
    - For the Ski Erg, maintain a fluid motion, using your legs to assist in the pull.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you're new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or focus on technique rather than intensity.
    - You can also substitute with a stationary bike or a light jog if equipment is limited.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with feet hip-width apart. Step up onto the box with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body up.
    - Bring the other foot up to meet the first on the box, then step back down one foot at a time.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and core engaged to maintain balance.
    - Ensure your entire foot is on the box to avoid slipping.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re still building strength or balance.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights or use a dumbbell for added resistance.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out. Place the kettlebell between your feet.
    - Hinge at the hips to grab the kettlebell with both hands. Stand tall, driving through your heels, and pull the kettlebell up to your chin, keeping your elbows high.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and chest up during the hinge.
    - Use your legs to initiate the movement, not just your arms.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re new to this movement.
    - If kettlebells aren’t available, you can substitute with dumbbell high pulls or even bodyweight squats.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to pull yourself up while keeping your body straight. Grab the rings with an overhand grip.
    - Lean back, keeping your body straight, and pull your chest towards the rings, squeezing your shoulder blades together.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
    - Avoid letting your hips sag or your shoulders round forward.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Adjust the height of the rings to make the movement easier or harder. Lower rings make it easier, while higher rings increase the difficulty.
    - If rings are unavailable, you can substitute with TRX rows or bent-over dumbbell rows.
