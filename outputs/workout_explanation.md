## Conditioning Segment [For Quality]

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - **Rowing:** Sit on the rower, strap your feet in, and grab the handle. Start with your knees bent and your back straight. Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest, then return to the starting position.
    - **Ski Erg:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, grab the handles, and start with your arms extended. Pull down with your arms while bending your knees slightly, engaging your core.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and core engaged throughout the movement.
    - For rowing, focus on driving with your legs first, then leaning back, and finally pulling with your arms.
    - For the Ski Erg, maintain a fluid motion, using your legs and arms in harmony.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you're new to rowing, consider reducing the intensity or duration to 2 minutes.
    - For the Ski Erg, you can also lower the resistance to make it easier.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand in front of the box with your feet hip-width apart. Step up with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body onto the box. Step down carefully and alternate legs.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core as you step up.
    - Make sure your entire foot is on the box to avoid slipping.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - If you need to reduce the load, you can perform step-ups without weights.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out. Hold the kettlebell with both hands in front of you. Hinge at your hips and lower the kettlebell to the ground, then explosively extend your hips and pull the kettlebell up to your chin, leading with your elbows.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back flat and core tight during the hinge.
    - Ensure your elbows are higher than your wrists at the top of the pull.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re still mastering the movement.
    - You can also perform the movement with a dumbbell if kettlebells are not available.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to lean back with your arms extended. Grab the rings and walk your feet forward until your body is at an angle. Pull your chest to the rings while keeping your body straight.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your core engaged and avoid sagging your hips.
    - Squeeze your shoulder blades together as you pull.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re unable to perform ring rows, you can substitute with TRX rows or bent-over dumbbell rows.
    - Adjust the angle of your body to make it easier or harder.
