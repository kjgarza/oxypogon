## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Start by sitting on the rower or ski erg with your feet securely strapped in.
    - For rowing: Grab the handle with both hands, keeping your back straight and core engaged.
    - For skiing: Hold the handles with a neutral grip, feet shoulder-width apart.
    - Initiate the movement by driving through your legs, then lean back slightly, and finally pull with your arms.
    - Reverse the order to return to the start position: arms extend, lean forward, and bend your knees.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and avoid rounding your shoulders.
    - Use your legs to drive the movement; your arms should follow.
    - Maintain a steady rhythm; don’t rush!

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or even 1:30 to focus on form.
    - Consider using a lighter resistance setting on the ski erg.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with your feet hip-width apart.
    - Step up onto the box with one foot, driving through your heel to lift your body up.
    - Bring your other foot up to meet the first on top of the box.
    - Step back down one foot at a time, maintaining control.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and core engaged throughout the movement.
    - Use your legs to power the step-up, not your back.
    - Alternate the leading foot each round to ensure balanced work.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re unsure about your balance or strength.
    - If stepping is challenging, you can perform seated leg lifts instead.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out.
    - Place the kettlebell between your feet and hinge at the hips to grab it with both hands.
    - Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up along your body to your chin.
    - Elbows should be high and outside as you pull.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and chest up during the lift.
    - Engage your core to protect your lower back.
    - Control the kettlebell on the way down; don’t let it drop.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell or perform the movement with a dumbbell.
    - If you’re new to this movement, practice with just the kettlebell or a PVC pipe to master the form.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to pull yourself up comfortably.
    - Grab the rings with an overhand grip and lean back, keeping your body straight.
    - Pull your chest towards the rings, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
    - Lower yourself back down with control.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
    - Avoid letting your hips sag or your shoulders round forward.
    - Focus on pulling with your back, not just your arms.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, perform inverted rows on a bar or use a TRX strap.
    - You can also perform seated rows with a resistance band.
