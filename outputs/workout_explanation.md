## Workout Overview

## Workout Breakdown

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Start by sitting on the rower or Ski Erg with your feet secured.
    - For the rower, grab the handle with both hands, keeping your back straight and core engaged.
    - Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle towards your chest, then return to the starting position.
    - For the Ski Erg, hold the handles with both hands, hinge slightly at the hips, and pull down while driving your elbows back, engaging your core.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and avoid rounding your shoulders.
    - Use your legs to drive the movement, not just your arms.
    - Maintain a steady rhythm; don’t rush!

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or even 1:30 to focus on form.
    - You can also substitute with a stationary bike or a light jog if equipment is limited.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand in front of the box with your feet hip-width apart.
    - Step up onto the box with one foot, driving through your heel to lift your body up.
    - Bring your other foot up to meet the first on the box, then step back down one foot at a time.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and core tight throughout the movement.
    - Ensure your entire foot is on the box to avoid slipping.
    - Alternate legs for each rep to ensure balanced strength.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - If you have knee issues, consider doing step-ups to a lower height or even just bodyweight squats.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out.
    - Place the kettlebell between your feet and hinge at the hips to grab the handle.
    - Drive through your heels to lift the kettlebell, pulling it up along your body to your chin, keeping your elbows high.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back flat and core engaged during the lift.
    - Use your legs to initiate the movement; your arms should be guides, not the main drivers.
    - Control the kettlebell on the way down to avoid injury.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re new to this movement or if your form is breaking down.
    - You can also perform a kettlebell deadlift (just lifting the kettlebell without the high pull) for fewer reps.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings to a height where you can comfortably pull yourself up.
    - Grab the rings with an overhand grip and lean back, keeping your body straight.
    - Pull your chest towards the rings, squeezing your shoulder blades together, then lower back down.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels; avoid sagging or arching your back.
    - Engage your core and don’t let your hips drop.
    - Focus on a controlled movement rather than rushing through the reps.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows on a bar or use a TRX.
    - Adjust the angle of your body to make it easier or harder—more horizontal is harder, more vertical is easier.
