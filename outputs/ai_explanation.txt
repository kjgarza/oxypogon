## Workout Overview

Hey everyone! Today’s WOD is all about quality and maintaining good movement patterns. We’ll be doing 6 rounds of a mix of cardio, strength, and bodyweight movements. The goal here is to focus on form and control rather than just speed. This will help build endurance and strength while keeping our bodies safe. Let’s break it down!

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - For the Row: Sit on the rower, strap your feet in, and grab the handle. Start with your knees bent, back straight, and arms extended.
    - Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest, keeping your elbows close.
    - Reverse the motion by extending your arms, leaning forward, and bending your knees.
    - For the Ski Erg: Stand tall, grip the handles, and start with your arms overhead. Pull down while bending your knees slightly, then return to the start.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your core engaged to protect your back.
    - Use your legs to drive the movement; your arms should follow.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing, consider reducing the time to 2:00.
    - For the Ski Erg, you can also switch to a rower if that feels more comfortable.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with your feet hip-width apart.
    - Step up with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body onto the box.
    - Bring your other foot up to meet the first, then step back down one foot at a time.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core throughout the movement.
    - Make sure your entire foot is on the box before stepping down.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights to focus on balance and control.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out.
    - Place the kettlebell between your feet. Hinge at your hips and grab the kettlebell with both hands.
    - Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up along your body to your chin, leading with your elbows.
    - Lower the kettlebell back down to the ground in a controlled manner.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and chest up during the lift.
    - Ensure the kettlebell stays close to your body throughout the movement.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re still mastering the movement.
    - If kettlebells are unavailable, you can substitute with dumbbells or perform a bodyweight squat.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings to a height that allows you to lean back comfortably while holding them.
    - Grab the rings, lean back, and keep your body straight from head to heels.
    - Pull your chest towards the rings while keeping your elbows close to your body, then lower back down.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Engage your core to maintain a straight line from head to heels.
    - Avoid using momentum; focus on a controlled pull.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows using a barbell or a TRX.
    - You can also do bodyweight rows on a low bar or a sturdy table.

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat:** 
    - Row/Ski Erg: 2:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 16/8 (use no weight)
    - Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls: 12kg/6kg
    - Ring Rows: 8 reps

- **Train:**
    - Row/Ski Erg: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 20/12 (light weight if comfortable)
    - Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls: 16kg/8kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

- **Compete:**
    - Row/Ski Erg: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 24/16 (moderate weight)
    - Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls: 24kg/16kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

### Strategy Advice

- Focus on maintaining a steady pace throughout the workout. It’s better to go slower and keep your form than to rush and risk injury.
- Break up the movements into manageable sets if needed, especially on the kettlebell pulls and ring rows.
- Remember to breathe and stay hydrated!
