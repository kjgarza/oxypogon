## Workout Overview

Hey everyone! Today’s WOD is structured for quality and consists of 6 rounds of four different movements. The goal here is to focus on maintaining good form and technique while building endurance and strength. We’ll be using the rower or ski erg for some cardio, followed by box step-ups, kettlebell sumo deadlift high pulls, and ring rows. Each movement is designed to challenge different muscle groups while keeping your heart rate up. Let’s break it down!

---

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

**Technique Steps:**
- **Rowing:** Sit on the rower with your feet strapped in. Grab the handle with both hands, keeping your arms straight. Start with your knees bent and your body leaning slightly forward.
- Push through your legs to extend them, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest while keeping your elbows close.
- Reverse the motion: extend your arms, lean forward, and bend your knees to return to the starting position.
  
- **Ski Erg:** Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Grab the handles with both hands, keeping your arms straight.
- Initiate the movement by pulling down with your arms while simultaneously bending your knees slightly and engaging your core.
- Return to the starting position by extending your arms and allowing your body to reset.

**Form Tips:**
- Keep your back straight and core engaged throughout the movement.
- Avoid overextending your back; focus on using your legs and arms in a fluid motion.

**Scaling/Modifications:**
- If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or even 1:30 to focus on form.
- You can also substitute with a stationary bike for a similar cardio effect.

---

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

**Technique Steps:**
- Stand facing the box with your feet hip-width apart.
- Step up onto the box with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body up.
- Bring the other foot up to meet the first on the box, then step back down one foot at a time.

**Form Tips:**
- Keep your chest up and core tight to maintain balance.
- Use your legs to lift, not your back; avoid leaning forward excessively.

**Scaling/Modifications:**
- Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
- If you have knee issues, you can perform step-ups without weights or do bodyweight squats instead.

---

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

**Technique Steps:**
- Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out, with the kettlebell on the floor between your feet.
- Hinge at your hips and bend your knees to grab the kettlebell with both hands.
- Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up along your body to your chin, keeping your elbows high.

**Form Tips:**
- Keep your back flat and chest up during the lift.
- Ensure the kettlebell stays close to your body throughout the movement.

**Scaling/Modifications:**
- Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re new to this movement.
- If kettlebells are unavailable, you can substitute with dumbbell high pulls.

---

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

**Technique Steps:**
- Set the rings at a height where you can comfortably reach them while standing.
- Grab the rings with an overhand grip and lean back, keeping your body straight.
- Pull your chest towards the rings by bending your elbows and squeezing your shoulder blades together.

**Form Tips:**
- Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels; avoid sagging your hips.
- Engage your core to maintain stability throughout the movement.

**Scaling/Modifications:**
- If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows on a barbell or use a TRX strap.
- Adjust the angle of your body to make it easier or harder based on your strength level.

---

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat:** 
  - Row/Ski: 2:00
  - Box Step-Ups: 16/8 (use bodyweight)
  - Kettlebell: 16kg/8kg
  - Ring Rows: 8 reps

- **Train:** 
  - Row/Ski: 3:00
  - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps (use a light weight)
  - Kettlebell: 20kg/12kg
  - Ring Rows: 10 reps

- **Compete:** 
  - Row/Ski: 3:00
  - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps (use a moderate weight)
  - Kettlebell: 24kg/16kg
  - Ring Rows: 10 reps

### Strategy Advice:
- Focus on maintaining a steady pace throughout the workout. It’s better to break up the reps into manageable sets rather than going all out and burning out early.
- Pay attention to your form, especially as you start to fatigue. Quality over quantity is key here!
