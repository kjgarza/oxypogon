## Workout Overview

Hey everyone! Today’s WOD is all about quality over quantity, focusing on building endurance and strength through a series of movements that will challenge your cardiovascular system and muscular endurance. We’ll be doing 6 rounds of rowing or skiing, followed by box step-ups, kettlebell sumo deadlift high pulls, and ring rows. The goal here is to maintain good form throughout each movement while keeping a steady pace. Let’s break it down step by step!

---

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

#### Technique Steps:
- **Setup**: Adjust the damper setting on the rower or ski erg to a comfortable level. Sit tall with your feet secured.
- **Rowing**: Start with your arms extended, engage your core, and initiate the movement by pushing through your legs. Lean back slightly as you pull the handle towards your chest, then extend your arms and lean forward to reset.
- **Ski Erg**: Stand tall, grip the handles, and initiate the movement by pulling down with your arms while driving your knees up slightly. Keep your core engaged throughout.

#### Form Tips:
- Keep your back straight and avoid rounding your shoulders.
- Use your legs to drive the movement; your arms should follow.
- Maintain a steady rhythm; don’t rush!

#### Scaling/Modifications:
- If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or even 1:30 to focus on form.
- Consider switching to a stationary bike if you have any discomfort.

---

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

#### Technique Steps:
- **Setup**: Stand facing the box with your feet hip-width apart.
- **Movement**: Step up onto the box with one foot, driving through your heel. Bring the other foot up to meet it on the box, then step back down one foot at a time.
- **Repetition**: Alternate legs for each rep.

#### Form Tips:
- Keep your chest up and core engaged throughout the movement.
- Ensure your entire foot is on the box to avoid slipping.
- Use your arms for balance if needed.

#### Scaling/Modifications:
- Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
- If you have knee issues, consider doing step-ups without weights or perform a seated leg lift instead.

---

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

#### Technique Steps:
- **Setup**: Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out, with the kettlebell on the ground between your feet.
- **Movement**: Hinge at your hips to grab the kettlebell with both hands. Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up along your body to your chin, keeping your elbows high.
- **Repetition**: Lower the kettlebell back down to the ground and repeat.

#### Form Tips:
- Keep your back straight and engage your lats as you pull.
- Avoid using your arms too much; focus on driving through your legs.
- Maintain a fluid motion from the deadlift to the high pull.

#### Scaling/Modifications:
- Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re new to this movement.
- If kettlebells are unavailable, you can substitute with dumbbells or perform a bodyweight squat.

---

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

#### Technique Steps:
- **Setup**: Adjust the rings to a height where you can comfortably reach them while standing. Grab the rings with an overhand grip.
- **Movement**: Lean back, keeping your body straight, and pull your chest towards the rings, squeezing your shoulder blades together at the top.
- **Repetition**: Lower yourself back down with control.

#### Form Tips:
- Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
- Avoid letting your hips sag or your shoulders round.
- Engage your core throughout the movement.

#### Scaling/Modifications:
- If rings are too challenging, perform inverted rows on a bar or use a TRX.
- If you’re still building strength, reduce the number of reps or perform them from a higher angle.

---

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat**: 
  - Row/Ski: 2:00
  - Box Step-Ups: 16/8 (lower box)
  - Kettlebell: 16/8 kg
  - Ring Rows: 8 reps

- **Train**: 
  - Row/Ski: 3:00
  - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps
  - Kettlebell: 20/12 kg
  - Ring Rows: 10 reps

- **Compete**: 
  - Row/Ski: 3:00
  - Box Step-Ups: 24/16 (standard height)
  - Kettlebell: 24/16 kg
  - Ring Rows: 10 reps

### Strategy Advice:
- Focus on maintaining a steady pace throughout the workout. It’s better to go slower and keep good form than to rush and risk injury.
- Break up the movements if needed, especially on the kettlebell high pulls and ring rows, to maintain quality.
- Stay hydrated and listen to your body; if something feels off, don’t hesitate to scale back.
