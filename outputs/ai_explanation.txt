## Workout Overview

Hey team! Today’s WOD is designed to build endurance and strength while keeping our movements smooth and controlled. We’ll be working through 6 rounds of a variety of exercises, focusing on quality over quantity. The goal here is to maintain good form throughout each movement, ensuring we’re not just getting through the workout, but really honing our technique and building our fitness.

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - For the Row: Sit on the rower with your feet secured. Start with your knees bent and arms extended. Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle towards your chest, keeping your elbows close.
    - For the Ski Erg: Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, grasp the handles with both hands. Start with a slight bend in your knees, then pull down and back with your arms while driving your hips forward.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and core engaged during the row.
    - In the Ski Erg, focus on using your legs and core to drive the movement, not just your arms.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or adjust the resistance to a lower setting.
  
### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with feet hip-width apart. Step up onto the box with one foot, driving through the heel to stand tall on the box. Step back down and repeat on the other leg.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core. Make sure your entire foot is on the box before standing up.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height or if you’re new to this movement.
  
### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with feet wider than shoulder-width, toes slightly pointed out. Hold the kettlebell with both hands between your legs. Hinge at the hips, keeping your back straight, then explosively extend your hips and pull the kettlebell up to your chin, leading with your elbows.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep the kettlebell close to your body and ensure your elbows stay above your wrists during the pull.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell or perform a standard deadlift if you’re not comfortable with the high pull.
  
### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height where you can comfortably reach them while standing. Grab the rings with an overhand grip, lean back with your body straight, and pull your chest towards the rings, keeping your elbows close to your body.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Engage your core and avoid sagging your hips. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  
- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, perform inverted rows on a barbell or use a TRX strap.

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat:** 
    - Row/Ski: 16/8 calories
    - Box Step-Ups: Use a lower box (12-16 inches)
    - Kettlebell: 12kg/8kg
    - Ring Rows: Adjust angle for easier pull
  
- **Train:** 
    - Row/Ski: 20/12 calories
    - Box Step-Ups: Standard box height (20 inches)
    - Kettlebell: 16kg/12kg
    - Ring Rows: Standard
  
- **Compete:** 
    - Row/Ski: 24/16 calories
    - Box Step-Ups: Standard box height (24 inches)
    - Kettlebell: 24kg/16kg
    - Ring Rows: Standard

### Strategy Advice

As you move through this workout, focus on maintaining a steady pace. It’s easy to rush through, but remember, we’re prioritizing quality. Break up the sets if needed, especially on the kettlebell high pulls and ring rows, to maintain good form. Keep your transitions smooth and use the rest time wisely to recover for the next round.

Let’s have fun with this one, and remember to listen to your body! If something feels off, don’t hesitate to scale or modify. You’ve got this!
