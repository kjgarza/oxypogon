## Workout Overview

Hey everyone! Today’s WOD is designed to build your aerobic capacity and muscular endurance while focusing on quality movement. We’ll be completing 6 rounds of a combination of rowing or skiing, box step-ups, kettlebell sumo deadlift high pulls, and ring rows. The goal here is to maintain good form throughout each movement and to challenge yourself without sacrificing technique. Remember, quality over quantity!

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Start by sitting comfortably on the rower or ski erg, feet secured.
    - For rowing, grab the handle with both hands, arms extended, and lean slightly forward.
    - For skiing, hold the handles with a neutral grip, standing tall.
    - Initiate the movement by driving through your legs, engaging your core, and pulling with your arms.
    - Focus on a smooth, controlled pace, aiming for consistent strokes.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and core engaged throughout the movement.
    - For rowing, ensure your elbows stay close to your body during the pull.
    - For skiing, maintain a strong posture and avoid leaning too far forward or backward.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you're new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or focus on technique rather than speed.
    - You can also substitute with a stationary bike or a light jog if equipment is limited.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with feet hip-width apart.
    - Step up onto the box with one foot, driving through your heel to lift your body.
    - Bring your other foot up to meet the first on the box, then step back down one foot at a time.
    - Alternate legs for each rep.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core as you step up.
    - Ensure your entire foot is on the box to avoid any missteps.
    - Use a box height that allows you to maintain control and stability.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re still building strength or balance.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights or use a bench for added stability.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out.
    - Place the kettlebell between your feet and hinge at the hips to grab it with both hands.
    - Drive through your heels, extending your hips and knees to lift the kettlebell.
    - As the kettlebell rises, pull it up towards your chin, keeping your elbows high and outside.
    - Lower the kettlebell back down in a controlled manner.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Maintain a neutral spine and avoid rounding your back.
    - Focus on using your legs and hips to generate power, not just your arms.
    - Keep the kettlebell close to your body throughout the movement.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell or perform the movement with a dumbbell.
    - If you’re new to this movement, practice the deadlift motion without the pull first.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to pull your body up comfortably.
    - Grab the rings with an overhand grip and lean back, keeping your body straight.
    - Pull your chest towards the rings by bending your elbows and squeezing your shoulder blades together.
    - Lower yourself back down in a controlled manner.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your body straight from head to heels; avoid sagging or arching your back.
    - Engage your core throughout the movement to maintain stability.
    - Focus on a full range of motion, pulling all the way to your chest.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If rings are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows on a barbell or use a TRX.
    - Adjust the angle of your body to make it easier or harder, depending on your strength level.

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat:** 
    - Row/Ski: 2:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 16”/8” box
    - Kettlebell: 16kg/8kg
    - Ring Rows: 8 reps

- **Train:** 
    - Row/Ski: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 20”/12” box
    - Kettlebell: 20kg/12kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

- **Compete:** 
    - Row/Ski: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 24”/16” box
    - Kettlebell: 24kg/16kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

### Strategy Advice

As you tackle this WOD, focus on maintaining a steady pace throughout each round. It’s better to break up the reps into manageable sets rather than going all out and burning out early. Listen to your body, and if you need to take a quick breather, do it! Quality movement is key, so prioritize your form over speed.

Let’s get after it today, and remember to have fun while pushing your limits!
