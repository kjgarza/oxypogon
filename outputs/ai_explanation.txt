## Workout Overview

Hey everyone! Today’s WOD is all about quality movement and building endurance. We’re going to tackle six rounds of rowing or skiing, followed by some box step-ups, kettlebell sumo deadlift high pulls, and ring rows. The goal here is to maintain good form throughout each movement while focusing on your breathing and pacing. Remember, it’s not about speed; it’s about executing each rep with precision and control.

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - For the Row: Sit on the rower with your feet secured. Grab the handle with both hands, keeping your arms straight and your back straight. Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle to your chest.
    - For the Ski Erg: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, grab the handles, and pull down with your arms while driving your knees slightly forward. Engage your core and keep your back straight.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your core engaged to protect your lower back.
    - In the row, focus on a smooth leg drive followed by a strong pull with your arms.
    - In the ski erg, use your legs to assist your arms, creating a fluid motion.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or focus on technique rather than intensity.
    - Use a lighter resistance setting on the Ski Erg if needed.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand facing the box with your feet hip-width apart. Step up with one foot, pressing through your heel to lift your body onto the box. Bring your other foot up to meet it, then step back down one foot at a time.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core throughout the movement.
    - Make sure your entire foot is on the box before stepping down to avoid any missteps.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - If balance is an issue, hold onto a wall or a sturdy object for support.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, kettlebell on the ground between your feet. Hinge at your hips and grab the kettlebell with both hands. Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up to your chin while keeping your elbows high.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and chest up as you lift.
    - Use your legs to drive the movement, not just your arms.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell if you’re still mastering the movement.
    - If kettlebells are unavailable, you can substitute with dumbbells or perform a bodyweight movement like air squats.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height where you can comfortably reach them. Grab the rings and lean back, keeping your body straight. Pull your chest towards the rings while keeping your elbows close to your body.

- **Form Tips:**
    - Maintain a straight line from your head to your heels.
    - Engage your core and avoid sagging your hips.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If ring rows are too challenging, you can perform inverted rows using a barbell or a sturdy table.
    - Alternatively, you can do bent-over dumbbell rows.

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat:** 
    - Row/Ski: 2:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 16/8 inch box
    - Kettlebell: 16/8 kg
    - Ring Rows: 8 reps

- **Train:** 
    - Row/Ski: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps on a 20/12 inch box
    - Kettlebell: 20/12 kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

- **Compete:** 
    - Row/Ski: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps on a 24/16 inch box
    - Kettlebell: 24/16 kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

### Strategy Advice

As you move through this WOD, focus on maintaining a steady pace. It’s easy to rush through the rounds, but remember that quality over quantity is key. Break up the movements if you need to, especially the kettlebell high pulls and ring rows, to maintain good form. Listen to your body, and don’t hesitate to scale if you feel fatigued. Let’s have fun with this and push ourselves while keeping safety in mind!
