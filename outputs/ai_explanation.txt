## Workout Overview

Hey team! Today’s WOD is all about quality and consistency. We’re diving into a six-round workout that combines some cardio, strength, and bodyweight movements. The goal here is to maintain good form throughout each round while pushing your limits. We’ll be working on our endurance with the row or ski erg, building strength with kettlebell movements, and improving our pulling strength with ring rows. Let’s break it down!

## Workout Components

### 1. Row/Ski Erg (3:00)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - **Rowing:** Sit on the erg with your feet strapped in. Start with your knees bent and your arms extended. Push through your legs, lean back slightly, and pull the handle towards your chest, keeping your elbows close.
    - **Ski Erg:** Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart. Grip the handles with both hands. Engage your core, and pull the handles down towards your hips while driving your arms back, using your legs to assist.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your back straight and core engaged throughout the movement.
    - For rowing, focus on a smooth, powerful leg drive followed by the pull.
    - For the ski erg, use your legs to initiate the movement, not just your arms.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - If you’re new to rowing or skiing, reduce the time to 2:00 or focus on technique rather than speed.
    - Consider using a lower resistance setting if available.

### 2. Box Step-Ups (20 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand in front of the box with your feet hip-width apart. Step up with one foot, driving through your heel to lift your body onto the box.
    - Bring your other foot up to the box, then step back down one foot at a time.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your chest up and engage your core as you step up.
    - Make sure your entire foot is on the box before standing tall.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lower box if you’re not comfortable with the height.
    - You can also perform step-ups without weights to focus on form.

### 3. Kettlebell Sumo Deadlift High Pulls (15 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out. Place the kettlebell between your feet.
    - Hinge at your hips to grab the kettlebell with both hands. Keep your back flat and chest up.
    - Drive through your heels to stand up, pulling the kettlebell up towards your chin, keeping your elbows high.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Maintain a strong core and flat back throughout the lift.
    - Use your legs to initiate the movement, not just your arms.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Use a lighter kettlebell or perform the movement without weight to focus on the technique.
    - If you’re unfamiliar with the movement, practice with a dumbbell or a PVC pipe first.

### 4. Ring Rows (10 reps)

- **Technique Steps:**
    - Set the rings at a height that allows you to pull your body up while keeping your feet on the ground. Grab the rings with an overhand grip.
    - Lean back with your arms extended, keeping your body straight. Pull your chest towards the rings, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  
- **Form Tips:**
    - Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
    - Avoid letting your hips sag or your shoulders round forward.

- **Scaling/Modifications:**
    - Adjust the height of the rings to make the movement easier or harder.
    - If rings are too challenging, you can substitute with inverted rows using a barbell or TRX straps.

## Loading and Rep-Modification Options

- **Sweat:** 
    - Row/Ski: 2:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 16/8 (no weights)
    - Kettlebell: 16kg/8kg
    - Ring Rows: 8 reps

- **Train:** 
    - Row/Ski: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps (bodyweight or light weights)
    - Kettlebell: 20kg/12kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

- **Compete:** 
    - Row/Ski: 3:00
    - Box Step-Ups: 20 reps (weighted)
    - Kettlebell: 24kg/16kg
    - Ring Rows: 10 reps

### Strategy Advice

As you tackle this WOD, focus on maintaining a steady pace. It’s better to keep moving with good form than to rush and risk injury. Break up the box step-ups and kettlebell pulls into manageable sets if needed. Remember, this workout is about quality, so listen to your body and adjust as necessary. Let’s crush this together!
